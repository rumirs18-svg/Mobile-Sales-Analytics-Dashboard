# Mobile Store Sales & Performance Analytics (Power BI)
An interactive Power BI business intelligence dashboard designed to monitor end-to-end sales performance, regional demand across India, customer rating patterns, and payment preferences for a retail mobile chain.

## Business Highlights & Key Metrics
- **Total Sales Revenue:** ₹769M across recorded transactions
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 19.15K units
- **Total Transactions:** ~4K transactions
- **Average Order Value:** ₹40K per transaction
---
## Core Visualizations & Analytics
1. **KPI Header Cards:** Quick tracking of overall sales revenue, units sold, total orders, and average transaction value.
2. **City-Wise Sales Map:** Geographic distribution across Indian hubs including Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Lucknow, and Bhopal.
3. **Monthly Demand Trend:** Line chart analyzing seasonality and volume shifts across months.
4. **Model Performance:** Bar chart detailing top revenue-generating smartphones (iPhone SE, OnePlus Nord, Galaxy Note 20, Vivo Y51, Galaxy S21).
5. **Customer Satisfaction:** Distribution curve showing product rating frequency.
6. **Payment Modes Breakdown:** Donut chart analyzing customer reliance across UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, and Cash.
7. **Daily Sales Distribution:** Area chart mapping revenue performance across days of the week.
8. **Interactive Slicers:** Year slicer (2021–2024), month navigation panel, and a single-click slicer reset button.
---
## Tech Stack & Tools
- **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling:** Power Query, Star Schema
- **Formulas:** DAX (Measures & Calculated Columns)
- **Mapping:** Bing Maps Integration
- **Dataset Source:** Retail Mobile Sales CSV
